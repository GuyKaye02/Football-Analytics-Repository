# Player Scouting Reports

This directory contains football scouting and analysis projects that combine
domain knowledge, video interpretation, and data-informed insights.

The reports vary in analytical depth and methodology, reflecting different
approaches to player evaluation.

## Types of Reports

### Data-Driven Scouting Reports
These reports emphasize event-level data, visualizations, and comparative metrics
to support player evaluation.

Example:
- **Fabián Ruiz — Euro 2024**
  - Event data analysis (StatsBomb)
  - Spatial involvement and passing under pressure
  - One-page, visualization-focused scouting profile

### Video & Tactical Scouting Reports
These reports focus on video analysis, telestration, and tactical interpretation,
supported by descriptive metrics where relevant.

Example:
- **Tammy Abraham — Scouting Report**
  - Video-based shot selection and movement analysis
  - Tactical role interpretation
  - Combination of broadcast footage, shot maps, and contextual metrics

## Notes
These scouting projects predate and complement my later work in probabilistic
modeling and machine learning (e.g. xG and action valuation models), and reflect
a long-standing interest in player evaluation and football decision-making.
