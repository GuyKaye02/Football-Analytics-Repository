# Player Scouting Reports

This directory contains football scouting and analysis projects that combine domain knowledge, video interpretation, and data-informed insights.

The reports vary in analytical depth and methodology, reflecting different approaches to player evaluation and different stages of my development.

---

## Types of Reports

### Data-Driven Scouting Reports

These reports emphasize event-level data, visualizations, and comparative metrics to support player evaluation.

**Example:**
- **Fabián Ruiz — Euro 2024**
  - Event data analysis (StatsBomb)
  - Spatial involvement and passing under pressure
  - One-page, visualization-focused scouting profile

---

### Video & Tactical Scouting Reports

These reports focus on video analysis, telestration, and tactical interpretation, supported by descriptive metrics where relevant.  
They reflect a more traditional scouting workflow and predate my later work in probabilistic modeling.

**Examples:**
- **Tammy Abraham — Scouting Report**
  - Video-based shot selection and movement analysis
  - Tactical role interpretation
  - Combination of broadcast footage, shot maps, and contextual metrics

- **Modern #9 — Tactical Case Studies**
  - Video-based analysis of striker profiles (e.g. Scamacca, Schick)
  - Telestrated clips highlighting movement, space creation, and role responsibilities
  - Focus on tactical principles rather than predictive modeling

---

## Notes

These scouting projects complement my later work in probabilistic modeling and machine learning (e.g. xG and action valuation models), and reflect a long-standing interest in player evaluation, football tactics, and decision-making.
